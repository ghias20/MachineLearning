Ensemble Technique:
    They are Machine Learning methods that combine multiple models usually Weak Learner to
    produce a single, stronger and more accurate model than any individual model alone.
Classified into 2 types:
    . Bagging
    . Boosting
Bagging:
 Multiple models are trained independently on different random samples of datasets and their
 predictions are combined to produce a final result.
 It is also called as Bootstrap Aggregation.
 Why do we need ??
 -> A Decision tree:
    . Have high variance
    . Easily overfits
    . Changes a lot if data changes slightly
 To overcome above problems 
 1. Bagging reduces variance & makes predictions more stable.
 How bagging Works??
 Bootstrap Sampling:
  1. From the original dataset, create multiple new sub datasets.
  2. Each dataset is created by random sampling with replacement
  3. Each Bootstrap dataset has same size as the original
 Note: (Outoff bag samples)
 Some data points repeat & some are left out, this is called out off bag samples

 Train Multiple models:
 1. Train 1 model perr Bootstrao dataset.
 2. usually it is a Decision tree
 3. Each tree is trained independently.
 Make predictions
    for regression-> Take avg
    for classification-> voting
 Final Outcome/Output

 Aggregated prediction is more stable and accurate than a single Decision tree

When to use Bagging:
1. When dataset is med to large
2. model is overfitting
3. Decision tree give unstable results.

### Boosting

Boosting is an ensemble technique where models are trained sequentially and each new model focus more on the mistakes made by the previous model.

### Why Boosting?

Boosting converts many weak learners into a strong learner.
### How boosting works?

1 Train first weak model 

2 Identify misclassified or high error points 

3 Give more importance to those points(misclassified) 

4 Train next model focusing on them

5 Repeat

6 Combine all models into a final prediction
